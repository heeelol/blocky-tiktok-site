Blocky What-Ifs

Blocky What-Ifs is a personal tool that makes short animated "what if" videos with blocky characters and uploads them as drafts to the creator's own TikTok account. The creator reviews every draft in the TikTok app before anything is published.

The app is used by its creator only. It does not read, collect or share anyone else's data.
