Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Blocky What-Ifs is a personal tool used by its creator to upload their own videos to their own TikTok account.

What the app accesses

When the creator logs in with TikTok, the app receives an access token and basic account identifiers from TikTok. These are used only to upload video drafts to that same account.

Where data is stored

The access token is stored locally on the creator's own computer. The app has no server, database or analytics, and it does not collect data from any other person.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or disclosed to third parties, other than the video files and requests sent to TikTok's API to perform the upload.

Deleting data

The creator can delete the locally stored token at any time and can revoke the app's access from TikTok's settings.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the creator through the GitHub account that hosts this page.
